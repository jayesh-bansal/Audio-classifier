# Messy Mashup — Music Genre Classifier

Upload a music clip and get predictions across 10 genres using an ensemble of CNN and CRNN models trained on Mel spectrograms.

**Supported genres:** blues · classical · country · disco · hiphop · jazz · metal · pop · reggae · rock

**Model:** Ensemble of 4-block CNN + CNN+BiGRU (CRNN) trained on synthetic mashup data with K-fold cross-validation.
